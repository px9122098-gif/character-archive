Satoru
Gojo

An impossible distance wrapped in confidence, humour, and electric blue.

Satoru Gojo is a special grade jujutsu sorcerer and a teacher at Tokyo Jujutsu High. Born with both the Limitless technique and the Six Eyes, he understands cursed energy with extraordinary precision and is widely regarded as the strongest sorcerer of the modern era.

His playful arrogance hides a serious purpose. Gojo teaches because power alone cannot rebuild the jujutsu world; lasting change requires a new generation capable of thinking beyond its inherited rules.

Infinity note

His signature contrast defines the archive: unreachable power presented with effortless humour and a disarming smile.
