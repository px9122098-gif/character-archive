Ichika
Amasawa

A playful smile that never reveals the whole plan.

Ichika enters the story as an energetic and teasing first-year student. Her relaxed manner is carefully deceptive: behind it sits a sharp observer who notices changes in people, reads a room quickly, and rarely acts without purpose.

Raised in the White Room's fifth generation, she combines exceptional academic ability with demanding physical training. Unlike characters who hide behind silence, Ichika turns unpredictability, humor, and confidence into part of her strategy.

Character note

Her greatest contrast is also her signature: warmth on the surface, calculation underneath.
