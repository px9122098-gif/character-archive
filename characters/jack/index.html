Actor profile · File 01

Jack
Wolfe

A performer who brings quiet vulnerability, musical instinct, and emotional intensity to every role.

Jack Wolfe is an English actor whose work moves naturally between screen and stage. He reached an international audience as Wylan Hendriks in Netflix’s Shadow and Bone and appeared as Tim Walker and Prince Tamino in The Magic Flute.

His theatre work has been equally defining. As Gabe in Next to Normal, Wolfe brought remarkable sensitivity and emotional precision to the role. He later made his Broadway debut as Orpheus in Hadestown.

Perfomance note

His performances often balance openness and restraint — a quiet vulnerability that can transform into an unmistakable stage presence.
